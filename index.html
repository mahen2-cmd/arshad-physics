FAQ

Questions students and parents ask

Which levels does he teach?

Cambridge O Level Physics (5054), IGCSE Physics (0625) and A Level Physics (9702), covering both AS and A2.

Where are the classes held?

He teaches at schools and academies across Lahore. Venues change by session, so message on WhatsApp for the current list.

How do I find out timings and fees?

Send a WhatsApp message to 0300 9412902 with your level and area. You will get the current batch timings, venues and fees.

Can I join in the middle of the session?

Message first. Whether you can join depends on where the batch is in the syllabus and how many seats are left.

Are his notes and workbooks used in class?

He is the author of the 286 Read and Write O Level notes and a series of O and A Level workbooks written around the Cambridge papers.

Can a school or academy hire him?

Yes. He is open to teaching at more schools and academies in Lahore, for regular classes, crash courses or exam revision. Use the “Contact for schools” button above.
