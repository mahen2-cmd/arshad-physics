About

30+ years of teaching A Level physics

Arshad Chaudhry has been teaching A Level Physics for more than 30 years. He holds an MSc in Physics and teaches O and A Level Physics in schools and academies across Lahore. He has taught at some of the city's best-known schools, including Aitchison College, LGS and Beaconhouse.

He is also the author of the “286 Read & Write” O Level Physics notes and a series of O and A Level workbooks used by students preparing for their Cambridge exams.

Over his career his students have earned World and National distinctions, but grades are not the only focus: he builds a real understanding of physics that stays with students for life.
